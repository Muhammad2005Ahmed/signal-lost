## Achievements in the SIGNAL LOST game

Players earn XP and rank up: Cadet at 0 XP, Technician at 100 XP, Engineer at 250 XP, Commander at 450 XP, and Station Legend at 700 XP.

First Contact gives 20 XP for entering the station.

Chatterbox gives 30 XP for asking A.R.I.A 5 questions.

Power Up gives 25 XP for restoring power to the first room.

Full Power gives 100 XP for restoring all four rooms.

Speedrunner gives 60 XP for reaching full power within 3 minutes of the first start.

Hidden Beacon gives 80 XP for discovering the secret project.

Curious Mind gives 40 XP for viewing every project in the Engineering Bay.

Linguist gives 30 XP for asking A.R.I.A a question in Urdu.

Code Breaker gives 70 XP for winning the Codebreaker mini-game.

Wire Master gives 70 XP for winning the Wires mini-game.

Survivor gives 60 XP for winning the Oxygen and Asteroids mini-games.

The Verdict gives 115 XP for passing the final quiz.
